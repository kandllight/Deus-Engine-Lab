Model assumptions

Displacement and mean piston speed are geometric calculations. Power uses estimated brake mean effective pressure (BMEP), a user-defined torque curve and an effective boost multiplier. Boost is total manifold gauge pressure, not the sum of parallel turbo ratings. Airflow assumes 25°C ambient air, ideal gas density and an approximate charge-temperature correction. Fuel flow uses user-entered BSFC. Materials and component selections are recorded but do not automatically certify strength or add horsepower. Detailed components are editable design inputs, not verified factory specifications. Cam duration is measured at .050 inch lobe lift; overlap assumes symmetric lobes. Spring load is a linear approximation. No knock, fatigue, compressor map or thermal simulation is performed.
