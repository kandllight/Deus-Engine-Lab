Model assumptions

Displacement and mean piston speed are geometric calculations. Power uses estimated brake mean effective pressure (BMEP), a user-defined torque curve and an effective boost multiplier. Boost is total manifold gauge pressure, not the sum of parallel turbo ratings. Airflow assumes 25°C ambient air, ideal gas density and an approximate charge-temperature correction. Fuel flow uses user-entered BSFC. Materials and component selections are recorded but do not automatically certify strength or add horsepower. Detailed components are editable design inputs, not verified factory specifications. Cam duration is measured at .050 inch lobe lift; overlap assumes symmetric lobes. Spring load is a linear approximation. Block spacing and overbore limits are provisional assumptions, not verified machining specifications. Inter-bore bridge is not measured wall thickness. No knock, fatigue, compressor map or thermal simulation is performed. Vehicle presets are approximate editable starting points, not verified trim specifications. Acceleration uses a simplified longitudinal model with assumed launch RPM, tire friction, load transfer, fixed gear ratios, shift interruption and drag. Top speed is a steady-state estimate capped by the model search range (335 mph). Braking assumes ideal traction and does not model fade or actual caliper force. Suspension frequencies use linear wheel rates; alignment, structural choices and component dimensions are recorded but not simulated. Net modification mass and final weight distribution must be entered manually. Swap classification is user-selected, not a fitment guarantee.
